07 — Questions
The fine print, plainly.

        
A dash means nothing has been read yet. A dash is not a zero.

      

      

        
Where do the fees wait?
In the VIBED vault, one public Solana wallet anyone can watch. Every coin launched with the VIBED line and fee share sends 100% of its creator fee there. It waits until that app's builder claims.

        
Who sends them?
For now, a person on the VIBED team. Once the tag is on the live page and the claim is recorded, they send the SOL to the wallet in the tag and keep forwarding new fees. Every payout is posted with its transaction.

        
What if the builder never claims?
It keeps waiting in the vault. It doesn't go to the launcher, and holders aren't owed it: nothing is promised to holders of any coin.

        
Can someone fake a claim?
They'd have to put the tag on the live homepage of the host in the coin's line, and anyone who can do that controls the page. We read that page itself. Screenshots, DMs and look-alike domains don't count.

        
My app is a single-page app. Does the tag still work?
Yes, if the tag is in your index.html. We read the HTML your server sends before any JavaScript runs, so a tag added later by a script won't be seen.

        
What is $VIBED?
The pad's own coin. That's all it is: no rewards, no burns, no share of anyone's fees. Nothing is promised to people who hold it.

        
Is VIBED affiliated with any AI tool?
No. VIBED isn't affiliated with or endorsed by any AI tool, app builder or host. And a coin that names an app isn't endorsed by that app's builder.
